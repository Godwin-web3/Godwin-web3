# GodwinXbt

**Smart Contract Engineer · Protocol Researcher · Security Researcher**

I build Solidity systems and investigate how protocols behave under adversarial conditions.

My work spans smart contract engineering, DeFi security, stablecoin and payment infrastructure, privacy, and protocol design. I focus on the mechanics beneath the interface: accounting invariants, trust assumptions, settlement paths, and the conditions under which systems fail.

I work from implementation and evidence—contract code, tests, transaction traces, on-chain data, and primary sources. The goal is not just to identify a bug, but to understand its real-world impact and verify the result.

## Focus

- **Engineering**: Solidity, EVM systems, protocol integrations, testing and proof-of-concept development.
- **Security**: Smart contract review, exploitability analysis, economic impact and adversarial testing.
- **Research**: DeFi mechanisms, stablecoins, payment infrastructure, privacy and cross-border settlement.
